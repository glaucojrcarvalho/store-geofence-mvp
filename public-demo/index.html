Trust & limitations

Coordinates are user-supplied and never verified. This example does not establish physical presence or prevent location spoofing.

Privacy-first by construction. Coordinates, demo tasks, and decisions stay in memory on this page. No network requests, accounts, actual GPS tracking, location attestation, or saved activity. All store and worker coordinates are illustrative.
